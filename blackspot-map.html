Known Black Spots

Click on a location below to open its details.

Kampala Northern Bypass High Risk

Location: Northern Bypass, Kampala

Past incidents: Routinely reported as one of Kampala's most dangerous stretches, with frequent collisions involving speeding vehicles, motorcycles (boda bodas), and pedestrians crossing outside marked crossings.

Notes: High traffic speed combined with limited pedestrian crossing points.

Kapchorwa District Road High Risk

Location: Kapchorwa District

Past incidents: Site of a serious school bus accident in July 2026, which resulted in multiple fatalities and injuries among school children.

Notes: Hilly terrain and sharp bends; extra caution is advised for buses and heavy vehicles.

Northern Uganda Highway High Risk

Location: Highway route, Northern Uganda

Past incidents: A bus-truck collision on this route resulted in multiple deaths, highlighting the dangers of long-distance highway travel at night or at high speed.

Notes: Poor lighting and long straight sections that encourage overspeeding.

Jinja Road Junction, Kampala Medium Risk

Location: Jinja Road, Kampala

Past incidents: Frequent minor collisions at peak hours due to heavy congestion and lane-cutting.

Notes: Congestion-related risk rather than high-speed risk.
