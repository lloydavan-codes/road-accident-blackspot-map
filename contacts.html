Police & Emergency Contacts

Photo: Traffic police on duty enforcing road safety.

If you witness or are involved in a road accident, contact the numbers below immediately.

National & Traffic Emergency Numbers
Service Number
National Emergency (Police / Ambulance / Fire) 999 or 112
Traffic Police Hotline (road emergencies & accidents) 0800 199 099
Police National Operations Centre 0800 199 699
Fire Rescue & Service Unit, Kampala 0800 121 222

Source: numbers published by the Uganda Police Force and the Government Citizen Interaction Centre. Always confirm the latest numbers on the official Uganda Police Force website (upf.go.ug) before printing or distributing this page, as hotlines can change.

What to do while you wait for help

  • Move to a safe place away from moving traffic if you can.
  • Do not move a seriously injured person unless they are in immediate danger (for example, fire).
  • Note the exact location so you can direct responders quickly.
